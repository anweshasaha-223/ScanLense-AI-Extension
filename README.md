# 🛡️ ScamLens AI Extension

### Check suspicious content before you act.

ScamLens AI is an AI-powered browser extension that helps users identify potentially fraudulent messages, links, offers, and online content while browsing.

Instead of repeatedly opening a separate website and copying suspicious content for analysis, users can simply **download the ScamLens extension once and use it whenever they need it**. This makes scam detection faster, easier, and more convenient for everyday browsing.

---

## 🚨 Problem

Online scams are becoming increasingly common and difficult to identify.

Users may receive:

- Phishing messages
- Fake offers
- Suspicious links
- Fake payment requests
- Impersonation messages
- Urgent or threatening messages
- Fraudulent websites

Many users are unsure whether the content they receive is genuine or a scam.

Traditional scam-checking methods can also require users to leave their current webpage, open another website, copy the suspicious content, and paste it for analysis.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**ScamLens AI** brings scam detection directly into the browser through an easy-to-use extension.

Users can install the extension once and use it whenever they encounter suspicious content.

The extension uses **Gemma 4** to analyze the content and provide an understandable risk assessment.

ScamLens helps users understand:

- 🔍 What looks suspicious
- ⚠️ Why the content may be risky
- 🧠 What scam patterns are detected
- 🛡️ What safer action the user can take

---

## ✨ What Makes ScamLens Unique?

### 🚀 1. Download Once, Use Anytime

Users do **not** have to repeatedly visit the ScamLens website whenever they want to check something suspicious.

They can simply:

**Download Extension → Browse Normally → Check Suspicious Content → Get AI Analysis**

This makes scam detection a part of the user's regular browsing workflow.

### ⚡ 2. Convenient Browser-Based Detection

Instead of interrupting their normal work and opening another website, users can access ScamLens directly from their browser.

This saves time and reduces unnecessary steps.

### 🧠 3. Explainable AI

ScamLens does not simply tell users:

**"This is a scam."**

It also explains **why the content may be suspicious** and highlights the warning signs detected by the AI.

### 👥 4. Simple for Everyone

The extension is designed to be easy to understand, even for users who do not have advanced cybersecurity knowledge.

---

## 🤖 Gemma 4 Integration

ScamLens uses **Gemma 4** as its AI intelligence layer.

Gemma 4 helps analyze suspicious content and identify patterns such as:

- Phishing
- Fake offers
- Impersonation
- Urgency tactics
- Suspicious payment requests
- Fraudulent messages
- Suspicious links
- Social engineering patterns

The results are converted into simple explanations that users can understand.
